# Loop Templates

## Open The Loop

### Goal Alignment
- Describe how this initiative aligns with strategic goals.
- Reference specific organizational priorities or OKRs.

### HLC Criteria
- Identify relevant Higher Learning Commission criteria addressed.
- Note standards, core components, or assurances of learning.

### Operational Tactic
- Outline the concrete actions, owners, and timelines.
- Include scope, dependencies, and resources required.

### Performance Measure
- Define the measurable indicator(s) to track progress.
- Include data source, frequency, and calculation method.

### Target
- State the desired threshold/benchmark for success.
- Include due date and any interim milestones.

---

## Close The Loop

### Performance Measure Results
- Report actuals vs. plan with dates.
- Summarize analysis of variance and key insights.

### Results Met
- Yes/No and brief rationale.
- Include evidence or citations to data.

### Continuous Improvement
- Actions taken or planned based on results.
- Update processes, training, or policies as needed.

### Future Budget Impact
- Identify budget changes (increase/decrease/reallocation).
- Note timing, funding sources, and cost-benefit considerations.
